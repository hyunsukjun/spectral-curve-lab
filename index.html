Spectral Shift · −2000 to +2000 HzMove spectral frequencies by a fixed Hz offset. 0 Hz preserves their positions.
Spectral Stretch · 0.5–2Compress or expand frequency spacing around 1 kHz. 1 is neutral. Playback speed and file duration stay unchanged.
Spectral Blur · 0–100%Smooth spectral changes over time. 0% is neutral; frequency positions and file duration stay unchanged.
Harmonicity · −100% to +100%Shape peak relationships: negative toward harmonic, positive toward inharmonic. 0% is neutral.
Spectral Freeze · 0–100%Hold a captured spectral state. 0% is off; 100% is full hold. This does not extend the file duration.
